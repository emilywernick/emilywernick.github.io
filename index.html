Emily is a digital artist and sociologist from Cupertino and a master's student at the CUNY Graduate Center. Finding joy in technical challenges and stylistic experimentation, their work reflects a dreamy blend of childhood wonder and the obscure. They spend their time between animation, designing, dreaming, and freaking out over technofeudalism.

instagram ↗ email me ↗

Informed by a background in sociology and anthropology, they draw inspiration from View-Master reels, video games, 1970s computer art, early web aesthetics, and 1990s CGI. Their work is an ongoing attempt to blend the dreamy, the nostalgic, and the uncanny.

You can browse their ever-growing inspiration board here.
